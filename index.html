About Mamak Mart

Mamak Mart is a walk-around shop game where you run your own Malaysian mamak shop. Walk your cook around the shop with one thumb, make teh tarik, roti canai and nasi lemak, and carry piles of food to hungry customers.

Stand on a yellow mat by a counter and the food is made by itself and stacks up in your hands. Walk to a customer to serve them, then pick up the coins they leave on the table. Spend coins by standing on coin circles to add tables, new food like Milo ais, mee goreng, murtabak and cendol, helpers who serve for you, and faster shoes. Get ready for the breakfast rush and the busy after-Maghrib rush!

3 quick tips

  1. Look at the bubble over each customer: it shows what they want and how patient they are.
  2. Carry a mix of food so you can serve more than one table in one trip.
  3. Hire helpers early. They carry food to customers while you cook.

How to play

👆 Put your thumb anywhere and slide to walk. Let go to stop.

🍳 Stand on a yellow mat by a counter to make food. It piles up in your hands.

🙋 Walk to a hungry customer. The bubble shows what they want. Stand next to them to serve.

🪙 When they finish eating, pick up the coins from their table.

⭐ Stand still on a coin circle to spend coins on new tables, new food, helpers and more.

🌅 Watch out for the breakfast rush and the after-Maghrib rush. Lots of customers come at once!
