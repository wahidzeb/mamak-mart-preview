# Mamak Mart (private preview) by Zaib

Private preview of Mamak Mart, a walk-around shop game set in your own 3D Malaysian mamak shop. Walk with one thumb, stand on a counter mat to make teh tarik, roti canai and nasi lemak, carry piles of food to hungry customers and collect their coins. Spend coins to add tables, new food (Milo ais, mee goreng, murtabak, cendol), helpers and upgrades. Watch out for the breakfast rush and the after-Maghrib rush!

Not listed anywhere and hidden from search (noindex). Free, no purchases. Progress is saved on your device.
